# Posting calendar

Cadence: **2 posts/week. Hard cap — do not exceed.** Mid-morning US Eastern.
House style: no em dashes. End each post with a question to drive comments.

> **Why the cap:** the Jul 2026 engagement pull showed cadence is not the lever,
> lane is. Corrected 2026-08-22 across all 19 chart posts: contested lanes average
> **2,271 impressions and 8.5 comments**, volume posts **1,127 and 1.8**. The
> "3,000-3,700 and 8-17 comments" figures previously quoted here were the top of the
> contested shelf, not its average. The gap is real and is widest on comments (4.9x). Daily also
> trains the audience to skim. Two sharp, argument-driven posts beat five fillers.
> "Post daily to build an audience" is algorithmic-feed advice; it does not fit a
> data-expert franchise. If tempted to post a third in a week, hold it for next
> week or fold it into a deep-dive.

## Weekly template
Two CVE Insights chart posts (Tue + Thu), leading with the proven lanes. Volume is
**milestone-only**, never a scheduled slot. Wednesday is reserved for Empirical/work
posts, which sit outside this chart cadence and do NOT count against the 2/week cap.

| Day | Slot | Lead lane | Notes |
|-----|------|-----------|-------|
| Tue | Scoring / measurement | cna_spread · cna_metric_table · scorer_divergence · ridgeline · cwe_spread | The "CVSS is an opinion" franchise; highest comments/reposts |
| Wed | Empirical / work | (not a chart) | Company/research posts; separate audience-job from the chart franchise |
| Thu | Exploitation | kev · kev_severity · kev_timing · exploited_cwe · cwe_kev · waffle_exploit | What actually gets exploited vs what you'd expect |

## Rotation queue
Cycle within each lane so no chart repeats too soon:
- **Tue (scoring):** cna_spread → scorer_divergence → cwe_spread → ridgeline → cna_metric_table → (repeat)
- **Thu (exploitation):** kev_severity → exploited_cwe → kev_timing → cwe_kev → waffle_exploit → (repeat)

## Copy & angle playbook (what actually drives reach)
Reach is decided by the post copy more than the chart. Full rules in `content/planning/INSIGHTS.md`
(Copy formula). The essentials:
- **Open on a one-sentence opinion, not a stat.** Line one is an arguable claim; the
  number is line two. Opinion-first posts pulled 2-3.5k impressions with many comments;
  number-first ones pulled about half and near-zero comments.
- **End on a genuine, varied question.** It is the comment engine; never drop it, never
  reuse a formula ("how does your pipeline handle X?").
- **Track reposts + comments**, not impressions/reactions, as the success metric.
- **Authority + stakes play:** periodically pair a chart with an outside authority's
  warning ("even \[credible outsider\] is saying X"). The Jul 2026 JP Morgan post did this
  and outran every chart by 6x (**21,007 impressions, 41 reposts**). Never manufacture
  alarm the data does not support.
- **Carousels are proven, not an experiment.** Both document posts are top of class:
  J.P. Morgan **21,007** (best post ever) and the AI Summit carousel **2,964** (beaten
  by only two of 19 chart posts). Builder exists at `Talks/AISummit/build_li_carousel.py`.
  Default to a carousel when an argument needs more than one image to land.
- **The ceiling is set by format, not by rigor.** In the 2026-08-12 90-day pull, no
  chart post cleared 3,686 while three commentary posts cleared 12,000. If a finding
  deserves reach, consider shipping it as commentary or a carousel rather than as a
  chart with a caption.

## Off-cadence (does NOT count against the 2/week cap only if genuinely newsworthy)
Milestone/volume posts (pace, daily_rate, two_year_overtake, calendar_heatmap) and
one-off curios (foxit_batch) are **milestone-triggered**, not scheduled. A big KEV
week can still warrant a same-day exploitation post, but that replaces the Thu slot,
it does not add a third.

## Measurement protocol (read before quoting any number from the Log)

The Log's impressions column is a **mixed-age snapshot and cannot be compared across
posts.** Every figure was captured at whatever age the post happened to be on pull day:
the 2026-08-12 rows are weeks-matured, while the Aug 18 and Aug 20 rows were 4 and 2
days old when verified on 2026-08-22. J.P. Morgan grew 7x between two pulls. Any claim
of the form "X outperformed Y" drawn from that column is partly measuring **post age**,
not reach. Two rules follow.

**1. Comments are the age-robust metric. Use them for every cross-post comparison.**
Comments land in the first 48 hours and then stop; impressions accrue for weeks. So
comment counts from posts of different ages are comparable and impressions are not.
Comments are already the north-star metric (reposts + comments, see the Copy formula in
`INSIGHTS.md`), so this costs nothing. The lane finding survives the switch and gets
stronger: on comments, contested lanes (scoring / exploitation / data quality) average
**8.5 across 11 posts against 1.8 across 8 volume posts, a 4.9x gap**, where the same
split on impressions is only 2.0x and is age-contaminated. Four of the eight volume
posts drew zero or one comment.

**2. Every new post gets a D7 reading.** Seven days after posting, record impressions,
reactions, comments and reposts in the D7 table below, and then never revise that row.
A fixed-age column is the only one that can answer "is this working?" **D7 cannot be
backfilled.** LinkedIn does not expose historical snapshots, so no post before
2026-08-22 has a D7 reading and none ever will; the series starts empty on purpose. Do
not fill it by guessing from a later pull, and do not pre-write a row for a post that
has not shipped (same failure mode as the Log rule above).
